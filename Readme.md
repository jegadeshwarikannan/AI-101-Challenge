# 🚀 AI 101 — My 101-Day Applied AI Challenge

## About

AI 101 is my 101-day journey to strengthen my understanding of Artificial Intelligence and build practical AI-powered applications and automation projects.

Rather than just consuming tutorials, I aim to learn by building, experimenting, documenting, and sharing my progress.

## 🎯 Goals

- Build strong foundations in AI and Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Understand prompting, embeddings, RAG, and AI agents.
- Develop practical AI automation projects.
- Learn to evaluate, test, and deploy AI applications.
- Build a portfolio of hands-on projects.
- Develop a consistent habit of documenting what I learn.
